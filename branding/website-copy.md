**EN**

>
> Every order through the apps costs you about a fifth of its value. That makes sense the first time — the app brought you someone new.
>
> But the customer who has ordered eight times? You pay the same commission every time. And you still don't have his number.

## 6 — Built for how Egyptians order

**AR**
> ## بيفهم زباينك زي ما بيتكلموا
>
> - **فويس نوت** — الزبون بيبعت رسالة صوت، والأوردر بيتسجل.
> - **فرانكو** — بيكتب `3ayez burger`، بيتفهم.
> - **العنوان بعلامة مميزة** — «جنب صيدلية العزبي» تنفع عنوان.
> - **كاش وفكة** — معاه ٢٠٠ جنيه؟ الباقي محسوب ومكتوب للكابتن.
> - **أكتر من فرع** — منيو وأسعار ومناطق توصيل لكل فرع لوحده.

**EN**
> ## It understands your customers the way they actually talk
>
> - **Voice notes** — the customer sends a voice message, the order is recorded.
> - **Franco-Arabic** — he types `3ayez burger`, it's understood.
> - **Landmark addresses** — "next to El Ezaby pharmacy" works as an address.
> - **Cash and change** — paying with a 200? The change is calculated and passed to the driver.
> - **Multiple branches** — separate menu, prices, and delivery zones for each.

## 8 — Objections

**AR**
> ## أسئلة بتتسأل
>
> **«موظفيني مش هيعرفوا يستخدموه»**
> مش هيتعلموا حاجة جديدة. الأوردر بيوصل جاهز — الكاشير بيشوفه زي ما بيشوف أي أوردر.
>
> **«ده تطبيق؟»**
> لا. مفيش حاجة تتحمّل، لا لك ولا لزباينك. كله على الواتساب.
>
> **«هيتكلم مع زباينـي إزاي؟»**
> باسم مطعمك، بالمصري. اسمنا مبيظهرش في الكلام ده — دي محادثة مطعمك مع زبونه.
>
> **«بتاخدوا عمولة؟»**
> لا. اشتراك شهري، وسعر ثابت للأوردر بعد الحد المشمول. مكتوب فوق.
>
> **«عندي نظام كاشير، هيتعارض معاه؟»**
> منادم بياخد الأوردرات، مش بديل للكاشير. الربط بينهم ممكن نتكلم فيه لما تحتاجه.

**EN**
> ## Questions people ask
>
> **"My staff won't be able to use it"** — There's nothing new for them to learn. The order arrives ready; the cashier sees it like any other order.
> **"Is this an app?"** — No. Nothing to download, for you or your customers. It all runs on WhatsApp.
> **"How will it talk to my customers?"** — In your restaurant's name, in Egyptian Arabic. Our name never appears — that conversation belongs to your restaurant.
> **"Do you take commission?"** — No. A monthly subscription, and a fixed per-order price past the included volume. It's written above.
> **"I have a POS — will this conflict with it?"** — Munadim takes the orders; it doesn't replace your POS. We can talk about connecting them when you need it.

## What is deliberately NOT on this page

Leave these out. Each is a settled decision, not an oversight.

- **No results, percentages, or savings figures.** One client, zero results data. Every proof point is a promise until it's measured on a named client.
- **No logo wall, no testimonials, no counters** ("500+ orders processed"). All false today, and this buyer's network is small enough to check.
- **No competitor named anywhere.** «التطبيقات» covers all of them, carries no legal exposure, and stays true in any market.
- **"Commission-free" is never the headline.** Four competitors say it — it's the entry ticket, not the position. Ownership leads; commission-free appears in the pricing section where it belongs.
- **No «شات بوت», «منصة», «ذكاء اصطناعي», «غير محدود», «مجاني», or «أرخص».** Selling the mechanism invites the "is this safe with my customers" fear; the rest are banned outright in the voice guidelines.
- **The 3D sandwich builder does not appear on this page.** It's a minute-eight product, not a minute-one one. Leading with it makes us a novelty instead of an obvious business decision.
- **No urgency devices.** No countdowns, no "limited offer", no discount badges. The arithmetic does the pushing.
